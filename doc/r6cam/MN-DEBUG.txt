Internal Temp
`````````````

Camera temperature. Q: all sensors, overheat status, heat timer, unit.

Battery level
`````````````

Charge in % and time left (reliable after a 2 % drop).

Screenshot - 10s
````````````````

Close the menu and wait for the beep: saves ``VRAMn.BMP`` with ML's graphics and the LiveView image, not Canon's icons.

File Manager
````````````

From the **file_man** module: browse the card, copy, move, delete.
`````````````````````````````

`````````````
Lens info
``````````````
