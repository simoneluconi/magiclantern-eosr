Display
~~~~~~~

Anamorphic
``````````

Your anamorphic lens desqueezed, **1.25x to 2x**, on the screen and in the viewfinder, also while recording. With open gate 4:3 too. It applies when the menu closes. Pictures and movies don't change.

Red display
```````````

Screen and viewfinder in red, for night and astro: 100 to 12 %. Pictures and movies don't change.

Advanced settings
`````````````````

**Color scheme** for ML's own graphics; Pure Red is for the night.
