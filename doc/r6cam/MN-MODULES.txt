Modules
~~~~~~~

.. raw:: latex

    \helplabel{Module}

Extra features, loaded at start-up. **SET** turns a module ON; then restart the camera. **Q**: Module info, with how to use it.

* **file_man**: Debug > File Manager.
* **lua**: the Scripts tab.
* **opengate**: Movie > Open gate 4K.
* **fps24**: Movie > 24.00p.
* **alli**: Movie > ALL-I.

Trouble? Hold **SET** while switching on: no modules are loaded.

Scripts
~~~~~~~

From the **lua** module: the ``.lua`` files in ``ML/scripts``.

* **Q** on a script: **Run Script**. **Autorun** runs it at every power on.
* **Show console**: what the scripts print.
* Scripts that change camera settings or take pictures fail in this build.
