MOV/MP4 time limit
``````````````````

Longest clip, up to **1:59:59** up to 30p. 50/60p goes up to 59:59, high frame rate up to 29:59. Heat can still stop a clip earlier. Trim long clips on a computer.

Heat timer
``````````

**2x / 3x / 4x** slow down Canon's 4K overheat timer. The temperature sensors still stop the camera.

Open gate 4K and 24.00p
```````````````````````

From the modules **opengate** (the whole sensor height: 4096x2736 in 3:2, or 3840x2880 in 4:3 for anamorphic lenses) and **fps24** (true 24.00p in 4K UHD). One at a time.
