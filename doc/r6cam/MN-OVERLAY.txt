Overlay
~~~~~~~

On Canon's clean LiveView screen, also while recording. Not on HDMI.

Global Draw
```````````

Main switch for all of ML's graphics in LiveView: bars and overlays.

Cropmarks
`````````

Framing lines, from the bitmaps in ``ML/CROPMKS``. Q: choose one.

Spotmeter
`````````

Brightness of a small spot. Q: unit and position.

Overlay: exposure and colour
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

False color
```````````

A colour for each brightness level, to judge the exposure. Q: palette.

Waveform
````````

Brightness across the image: white at the top, black at the bottom. Q: size.

Vectorscope
```````````

Colours on a circle, neutral in the centre. For white balance and skin tones.
