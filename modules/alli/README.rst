ALL-I
=====

Adds Canon's own ALL-I (For editing) to Movie rec. quality, in 4K UHD and FHD up to 30p.

- Turn the entry ON, then choose "For editing (ALL-I)" in Canon's menu.
- Works in Canon Log and HDR PQ too (10-bit H.265).
- 4K ALL-I is 470 Mbps: use a V90 card.
- Turn it OFF in LiveView: the setting goes back to IPB.
- Not together with opengate.
