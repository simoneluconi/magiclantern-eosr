Open gate 4K
============

Records 4K at the full sensor height: 4096x2736 (3:2) or 3840x2880 (4:3,
for anamorphic lenses), at 25p, 29.97p or 23.98p. HEVC 4:2:2 10-bit with
Canon Log, or H.264.

- Set 4K 25p, 29.97p or 23.98p, movie crop off.
- Choose 3:2 or 4:3, go to photo mode once, then back to movie.
- The entry is OFF after every restart.
- FHD, 50p or more, or crop pause it. Back in 4K 25p, 29.97p or 23.98p,
  go to photo mode once: a box reminds you.
- Change size or frame rate in Canon's MENU. Turn it OFF before using
  the Q menu for them.
- No HDMI. Play the clips on a computer, not in camera.
- Not together with fps24.
